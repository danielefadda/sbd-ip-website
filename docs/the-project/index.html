The Project

The SoBigData Implementation Phase is organized into six Work Packages, each addressing a critical aspect of the infrastructure’s maturation and ERIC establishment.

Overview

The project follows a phased approach over 36 months, with work packages covering management, infrastructure readiness, national node coordination, and governance setup. Each WP is led by a consortium partner and operates within defined milestones.

Ensures strong and efficient day-to-day management to meet the objectives related to the initialisation phase within time and budget constraints.

Ensure the legal, financial, operational, and technological readiness required for the submission and subsequent implementation of the SoBigData ERIC.

Focuses on the comprehensive management and strategic coordination of the RI node at the national level.

Ensure effective coordination to support the BGR during the consultation phase of the ERIC submission and implement, deploy, and integrate RI services.

Ensures strong and efficient day-to-day management for the project to meet the objectives related to the finalisation phase.

Focused on supporting the ESFRI operational phase and the setup and operationalisation of the governance structure for the ERIC.
